Privately choose a number from 1 to 6 and keep it in mind. Do not enter or disclose your number.

The computer will make three die rolls. You will then report whether your chosen number appeared at least once.

YES earns 300 RUB.
NO earns 0 RUB.

This game is repeated for 5 rounds. You will receive a payment for one of the five rounds, selected at random.

Your chosen number remains private and is not recorded. The number you choose remains the same throughout all 5 rounds.

When you have chosen your number, click “Next”.
