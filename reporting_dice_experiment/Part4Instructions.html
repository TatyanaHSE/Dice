In this part of the experiment you will play a new game with another participant. The game has two roles: Sender and Receiver.

The computer will randomly divide all participants into pairs. Within each pair the computer will also randomly assign who will be the Sender and who will be the Receiver. You will not learn the identity of your partner, and your partner will not learn your identity.

Before the game starts you will choose a strategy for each possible role: one strategy if you are assigned the role of Sender, and one strategy if you are assigned the role of Receiver.

After strategies are chosen, the computer will randomly select one of the two payoff tables, Table 1 or Table 2. You will see these tables on the next screen. The Sender will then send one of the two predefined messages to the Receiver, indicating which option, A or B, gives a higher payoff to the Receiver. After seeing the message, the Receiver will choose Option A or Option B. The payoff for both participants is determined by the selected table, the Receiver’s choice, and the role each participant was assigned.

Your strategy, your assigned role, and the decisions made in the game will determine your earnings in this part of the experiment.

Click “Next” to begin selecting your strategies.
